## Data presentation rules

- Keep India Net, India Gross and Worldwide Gross as distinct measures.
- Do not add India Net to Overseas Gross to calculate Worldwide Gross; use India Gross + Overseas Gross when both are sourced on the same basis.
- Keep blocked-seat and excluding-blocked-seat advance-booking values separately labelled when a source provides both.
- Do not show an opening-day collection before theatrical release. Pre-sales remain under Advance Booking.
- Show the reporting date/time and source near every collection metric.
- Review each source and poster link before future refreshes; do not assume external image URLs remain stable.
