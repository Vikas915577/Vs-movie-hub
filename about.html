About VS Movie Hub

VS Movie Hub is an independent movie-information project tracking films in cinemas, advance ticket bookings, upcoming theatrical releases and yearly box-office highlights.

We aim to make each number understandable by showing its source, reporting time and collection basis. We are not affiliated with film studios, distributors, exhibitors or the box-office sources linked on this site.

Movie posters and promotional artwork remain the property of their respective rights holders and are credited to the linked source where available.
