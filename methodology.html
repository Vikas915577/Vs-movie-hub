HOW WE REPORT

Data Methodology

Collection types, booking figures and release information are kept separate.

India Net vs India Gross

India Net is the reported domestic box-office amount before GST; India Gross includes applicable taxes. Overseas Gross refers to collections outside India. Worldwide Gross should be used only where a source publishes it or the components can be verified on the same basis. We do not derive a worldwide total by mixing incompatible values.

Live estimates

Some running-film sources update during the day based on tracked shows. Those figures are labelled live estimates, with the reporting day and source update time. A live estimate can change before the final daily report.

Advance booking

Ticket-booking revenue is not box-office collection. We identify whether a source reports gross or net, whether blocked seats are included, the dates on sale, tickets and shows, and the snapshot timestamp when available. Different trackers may count blocked seats differently.

Upcoming releases

Release-calendar dates can change. We identify dates as official only when a production-house or exhibitor source confirms them; third-party calendar dates are labelled reported. No collection is displayed before a film releases.

Corrections

Each movie page links its data source. Please use the Contact page to report a broken poster, updated source or incorrect figure. We will review corrections and update the last-checked timestamp.
