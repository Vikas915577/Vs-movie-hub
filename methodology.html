Data methodology

This page will document how VS Movie Hub sources, labels and updates movie information.

Collection labels

India Net, India Gross, Overseas Gross and Worldwide Gross will be kept as separate fields. Figures from different reporting bases will not be added together.

Reported vs estimated

Every collection figure should be labelled as reported, estimated or independently calculated, with a source link and a last-updated timestamp wherever available.

Advance booking

Ticket sales, gross booking, show counts and occupancy will only be displayed when a credible source and reporting period are available.

The methodology will be expanded and applied consistently before live figures are published.
