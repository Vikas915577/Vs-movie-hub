SIDE-BY-SIDE ANALYSIS

Movie Comparison

Choose two or three titles. VS Movie Hub compares only matching India Net and India Gross measures where available.

Comparison is a snapshot comparison, not a matched-day comparison. A movie with a longer theatrical run can naturally have a larger cumulative total.

What is and isn't compared

India Net is compared with India Net; India Gross with India Gross. Advance-booking numbers are excluded because ticket pre-sales are not theatrical collections. Titles without a published India Net amount are not selectable.
